# Photo Recovery & Restore privacy policy

**Effective date: October 12, 2026**

This policy describes the current Android app behavior. Before publishing, the
app owner must host this policy at a stable public HTTPS URL, add that URL to
the Google Play listing, and replace or supplement the support-contact wording
with the developer's actual support contact if required by the listing or local
law.

## Summary

Photo Recovery & Restore is designed to process photo recovery data locally on
the user's Android device. The app does not upload photos, recovery copies, or
photo metadata to a server, and it does not include advertising or analytics
services.

## Information the app accesses

When the user grants Android photo access, the app reads image records and
limited metadata needed to find visible and system-trash records, compare
current media, classify recovery results, and prevent an existing photo from
being shown as deleted. When the user chooses a folder with Android Storage
Access Framework, the app can read only that folder and its permitted
descendants. Access is not used to browse unrelated apps or restricted Android
data folders.

## How information is used

Photo bytes and metadata are used only on the device to show recovery results,
create a user-requested restored copy, or protect a copy in Recovery Shield.
The app does not sell, rent, profile, or share this information with third
parties.

## Recovery Shield

If the user chooses Recovery Shield, the app stores a protected copy in
private app storage on the device. Shield copies remain local, can be removed
from the app, and are removed by Android when the app is uninstalled. Removing
a Shield copy does not delete the original photo.

## Sharing and retention

The app has no cloud upload or automatic sharing feature. Recovery results and
temporary processing data remain on the device until they are cleared, replaced
by normal app-cache cleanup, or removed with the app. A restored copy is
published to the Android media library only after the user requests the restore
operation.

## User choices

The user can deny or later revoke photo permission, limit access to selected
photos where Android provides that option, remove a persisted folder grant in
Android settings, clear Recovery Shield copies, or uninstall the app. Revoking
access can make some recovery features unavailable; the app does not treat
inaccessible files as deleted.

## Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer support contact shown on
the app's Google Play listing. This policy may be updated when the app's data
practices change; the effective date will be updated with material changes.
